Origins & Values

Our Story

Lithium House was established in Nowshera to eliminate market confusion and sub-standard installations. Seeing consumers burdened with mismatched batteries and counterfeit components, we pioneered genuine, tier-1 solar packages with laboratory-tested LiFePO4 battery storage, honest engineering advice, and dedicated local after-sales service right here on GT Road.

Long-Term Impact

Our Vision

To be Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s foremost center for clean energy innovation—empowering every home, hospital, commercial business, and agricultural tube-well with seamless, uninterrupted power through reliable solar panels and durable lithium energy storage systems engineered to last decades.

Daily Commitment

Our Mission

To deliver precision-engineered solar installations built strictly with certified Tier-1 equipment, transparent pricing, safe DC/AC protections, and comprehensive in-house inverter diagnostics—ensuring maximum energy generation, rapid ROI, and total peace of mind for our clients.
